Competition Rules

General Rules

  1. Teams must consist of 2-4 members
  2. Each team must register with a unique team name
  3. All team members must be present during the competition
  4. Teams must follow the honor code and not share solutions

Competition Format

  1. The hunt consists of multiple puzzles and challenges
  2. Each puzzle has a specific point value
  3. Teams must solve puzzles in order
  4. Time is a factor in final scoring

Scoring

  1. Points are awarded for each solved puzzle
  2. Bonus points are given for quick solutions
  3. The team with the highest score wins
  4. In case of a tie, the team that finished first wins

Important Notes

  • All decisions by the judges are final
  • Any form of cheating will result in disqualification
  • Teams must respect other participants and staff
  • Have fun and enjoy the challenge!
